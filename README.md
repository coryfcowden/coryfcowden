## Featured Product Design Work

### Farm-ng / Bonsai Robotics — Amiga Support Experience
*Support Intern · Summer 2025*

Independently designed issue-reporting experiences for **two distinct Amiga software stacks**. By reviewing customer support requests, Discourse discussions, and product documentation, I identified an opportunity to help operators troubleshoot common issues before contacting support.

I explored different interface approaches for each platform, including high-contrast controls and larger touch targets for a smaller display used in outdoor field conditions.

**[Figma designs](https://www.figma.com/design/MtXspyJpeWJPUjjQqfRHLv/Farm-ng-Debug-App)**

### Cáritas Valencia — Mobile Outreach App
*Cal Poly senior capstone · September 2025–June 2026*

Collaborated with Cáritas Valencia in Spain to help centralize fragmented workflows for technicians and volunteers documenting homeless outreach. I worked with stakeholders to understand existing processes and terminology, helped define requirements, designed high-fidelity interfaces, and led stakeholder usability testing alongside the development team.

I also created an alpha site to support testing, documentation, and continued development after the team's final handoff.

**[Figma designs](https://www.figma.com/design/lmt6Mqf4U66QEwbwV6pL0r/Caritas-Team-Library?node-id=2323-2)** · **[Project alpha site](https://caritas-mobile.gitbook.io/caritas-mobile-alpha-site)**

### Athleat — Student-Athlete Nutrition App
*Cal Poly team project · Fall 2025*

Designed and implemented the onboarding flow for a nutrition-planning app for community college student-athletes. Through interviews and usability testing with two athletes, plus heuristic evaluation, our team found that the original weekly meal-planning flow felt too rigid and confusing. We redesigned it to support more flexible planning.

I translated high-fidelity Figma prototypes into a working frontend.

**[Figma designs](https://www.figma.com/design/OkUoTMaKFstt3dYy038mjN/CSC-484?node-id=0-1)** · **[Team repository](https://github.com/Rayn322/athleat)**
